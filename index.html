UX Research Case Study · Midterm Project

Tracking the Campus Shuttle

An end-to-end UX research process — from empathy and observation to information architecture and prioritization — for one of the most common broken experiences on campus: not knowing when the shuttle will arrive.

Student: Parth Bhirwandekar Program: B.Tech AI & ML, Vijaybhoomi University Problem Space: Campus Shuttle Commute

Phase 1

Empathy & Definition

Before proposing anything, the goal here was to understand the user's world as it actually is — starting with assumptions, then replacing them with real observation and real conversations.

Step 1

Proto-Persona

Name

Aaryan

Background

Aaryan is a B.Tech student at Vijaybhoomi University. He takes the college shuttle bus every day.

Goals

  • Get to campus on time for lectures and exams.
  • Catch his train on time when going back home.

Assumed Current Behaviour

Aaryan thinks the bus usually comes between 8:00–8:15 AM and leaves by 8:20 AM. But this isn't fixed — the bus doesn't always follow this timing.

Frustrations

  • He has to stand at the bus stop for 15–20 minutes every day without knowing when the bus will actually show up.
  • Because the timing is unpredictable, he's often late for lectures and exams.
  • He also risks missing his train on the way back home.

Quote

"I never know exactly when the bus will arrive, so I end up either waiting or worrying about being late."

Step 2

Observation Log

Location: Campus bus stop Time: 8:00 AM – 8:30 AM

What Was Observed

  • The student reaches the stop at 8:00 AM.
  • He checks his phone again and again.
  • He keeps looking down the road to see if the bus is coming.
  • At 8:15 AM, he makes a phone call.
  • The bus arrives at 8:20 AM.
  • He boards the bus quickly.

Step 3

Interview Notes

Raw Responses From 3 Real Students

  1. "There is no fixed arrival time; the bus often arrives either early or late."
  2. "Sometimes the bus leaves campus late. One student missed their train and had to wait 30 mins for the next train."
  3. "There is limited space for seating and luggage on the bus, as it is shared by faculty members, admin staff, and students."
  4. "Sometimes the bus changes, which results in lack of seating due to difference in seating capacity between buses."
  5. "Most of the seats are occupied by staff; only a few seats are available for students."
  6. "The price is too high compared to local buses, since there is no difference in the quality or service between the two."
  7. "Students feel there should be separate buses for students and college staff."

Step 4

Real User Persona

Name

Aaryan

Role

B.Tech student at Vijaybhoomi University, commutes daily via the campus shuttle.

Goals

  • Reach campus on time for lectures and exams.
  • Catch the connecting train on time while returning home.

Observed Behaviour

  • Arrives at the bus stop early, waits without any confirmed timing.
  • Checks phone repeatedly while waiting.
  • Sometimes calls a friend to ask about the bus's location.

Facts Gathered

  • No fixed arrival/departure time for the shuttle.
  • A missed shuttle connection has caused at least one student to miss a train, resulting in a 30-minute wait.
  • The shuttle is shared with faculty and admin staff, resulting in limited seating.
  • Seating capacity differs between buses, sometimes forcing students to stand.
  • Shuttle cost is seen as high relative to local buses.
  • Students have expressed a preference for separate buses for students and staff.
